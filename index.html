For my Anu ♡

Seven months already… and somehow you still manage to be my favourite person to annoy, love, tease and care about all at once.

I know I have made mistakes, had moods, overthought things and sometimes made things harder than they needed to be. But through all of that, you stayed. You never really turned your face away from me, and that means more to me than I can properly put into words.

I just want you to know that I choose you. Not only on the easy days, but on the weird, messy and difficult ones too. I want us to keep learning each other, laughing at stupid things, making our own little world and collecting a million more moments together.

Happy seven months, meri Anuuu. Keep being my favourite girl, my soft corner and the person I somehow always want to come back to. ♡

— yours, always ♡
